PELLEXA’s Operational Model and Profile
PELLEXA specializes in sourcing, quality control, and international supply chain management for business-to-business (B2B) clients. Ultimate Goal: Reducing purchasing and import costs for the client (compared to a local importer or an existing supplier) through a controlled and calculated process (naturally, the process takes longer, but costs are reduced).

1. Framework and Entry Threshold
Large Quantities Only: Operations are strictly limited to full container load (FCL) volumes and above.

Controlled, Non-Instant Process: Cost reduction is a process that requires time, research, and verification; it is not executed on short notice or instantly.

2. Risk Mitigation and Gradual Verification Process
Risk management is not based on false promises, but on a meticulous and transparent workflow:

Approval of Specifications and Standards: Presenting the supplier's technical specifications, standards, certificates (and Kosher certifications, where required for food products) to the client.

Sample Approval: Production and dispatch of samples for client testing and approval.

Pilot Batch: When necessary, running a small trial batch to ensure the process flows smoothly.

Full Transaction: Transitioning to mass production only after complete approval of all preliminary stages.

3. Legal, Commercial, and Logistic Model
Transparency and Non-Circumvention / Non-Disclosure Agreement (NCNDA): Disclosing the original supplier and specifications to the client, while having both client and supplier sign NCNDA agreements.

Back-to-Back Contracts: A structured legal and commercial commitment aligning transaction terms, pricing, and timelines between the factory and the client.

Payment Mechanism: Standard payment terms of 30% / 70% or 100% in advance (synchronized back-to-back between client and supplier).

Logistics and Import Framework:

PELLEXA is not a freight forwarder, customs broker, or the official importer of record (in the vast majority of cases, we are not the importers, and it should not be presented as such).

Operations are conducted using the client's import license or via a local company handling imports / a local importer, working with recognized freight forwarders (or supplier forwarders) and customs brokers.

If the client is not an importer, a suitable solution is arranged via an importer company or the import department of a freight forwarder (if available).

Execution of Blind Shipping for institutional clients as needed.

4. Quality Control and Insurance
On-Site Quality Control (Third-Party QC): Conducting quality inspections through a third party. As a standard, 2 inspections are performed (at the start of production and upon completion at the packaging stage), with flexibility for 1 or 3 inspections depending on order complexity.

Insurance Coverage: Comprehensive ALL RISK insurance policy provided through a third-party insurance company.

5. Areas of Expertise and Product Categories
Raw Materials and Wholesale Food: Bulk procurement (food categories include matcha, industrial tea, cocoa, coconut, seed oils, rice, pasta, canned goods, and primarily dry food) through authorized suppliers only.

Industrial and Consumer Goods: Heavy equipment, construction materials, LED signs, acrylic products, specialized protective cases (such as TCG), and brand packaging. (Medical equipment is not part of the public/general product portfolio and is handled internally only on an ad-hoc basis with established contacts).
